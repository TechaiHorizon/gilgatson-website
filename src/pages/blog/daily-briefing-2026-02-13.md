**Anthropic's latest funding round includes part of the $15B commitment from Microsoft and Nvidia in 2025; sources say the remainder was several times subscribed**  
https://www.ft.com/content/d21f4583-a05d-4a94-8404-f1e02a332283  
Microsoft and Nvidia’s massive $15 billion commitment to Anthropic is being hailed as a clear endorsement of the startup’s AI future. But the fact that the remainder of the funding round was “several times subscribed” reveals an overheated market desperate to back AI unicorns regardless of fundamentals. This frenzy inflates valuations dangerously, setting the stage for a painful correction once the promised breakthroughs fail to materialize at scale. Strategic tech alliances are morphing into speculative gambling hubs under the guise of innovation.  

**GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark is OpenAI's first AI model to run on chips from Nvidia rival Cerebras; OpenAI says Codex has more than 1M weekly active users**  
https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-02-12/openai-debuts-first-model-using-chips-from-nvidia-rival-cerebras  
OpenAI’s pivot to Cerebras chips for GPT-5.3-Codex-Spark signals cracks in Nvidia’s dominance, but it’s premature to assume a tectonic shift. The million-plus weekly users touted mask the fact that true enterprise adoption remains limited, and switching hardware ecosystems midstream imposes enormous hidden costs and integration risks. This move is less about performance leaps and more about diversifying supply chains amidst escalating US-China tech decoupling pressures. Expect incremental gains, not an overnight revolution.  

**Trump administration reaches a trade deal to lower Taiwan’s tariff barriers**  
https://apnews.com/article/trump-taiwan-china-trade-deal-2b1743397ba33010463d41132b75ce53  
The Trump administration’s trade deal with Taiwan is framed as a win for US-Taiwan tech cooperation, but it dangerously escalates geopolitical tensions with China at a fraught moment. Lowering tariffs appears as economic pragmatism but risks entangling critical semiconductor supply chains with volatile cross-strait politics. The deal gambles that market access trumps security concerns, yet it may accelerate Beijing’s incentives to ramp up self-reliance—undermining the very cooperation it hopes to deepen.  

**OpenAI sidesteps Nvidia with unusually fast coding model on plate-sized chips**  
https://arstechnica.com/ai/2026/02/openai-sidesteps-nvidia-with-unusually-fast-coding-model-on-plate-sized-chips/  
OpenAI’s new coding model running on unconventional “plate-sized” chips looks like a daring play to escape Nvidia’s stranglehold. But these specialized chips come with trade-offs: reduced generality, integration complexity, and questionable scalability beyond niche workloads. This strategy underscores the risk that AI hardware fragmentation could stifle the industry’s progress, creating bottlenecks where innovation is throttled by incompatible tech islands rather than unified platforms.

Valuations at this scale are a flashing warning: the AI gold rush is primed for a shakeout.
